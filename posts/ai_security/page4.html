Blocking phrases is brittle. Attackers can rephrase their prompts in effectively infinite ways to bypass static filters.

Adaptive Attacks: If you block "Ignore", they use "Forget". If you block "Forget", they use "Disregard". It's a game of whack-a-mole you can't win.

Invisible Payloads: Some injections use hidden text, Unicode characters, or foreign languages that filters miss but LLMs understand.
